What Dr Dutta looks after

Most of her work is with newborns, especially babies born early or sick. She also sees infants and children for everyday illness, vaccines and development.

  • Neonatal intensive care

    Ventilation (HFOV, SIMV), CPAP, high flow and inhaled nitric oxide for babies who need help breathing.

  • Preterm and low birth weight babies

    From the first hours in the NICU to feeding, growth, eye and hearing checks, and the follow-up clinic.

  • Heart and circulation

    Bedside echocardiography and point of care ultrasound to guide treatment of shock, PDA and pulmonary hypertension.

  • Newborn brain care

    Therapeutic hypothermia, amplitude integrated EEG, seizure management and neurodevelopmental follow-up.

  • Feeding and nutrition

    Breastfeeding support, donor milk, fortification and parenteral nutrition for babies with complex needs.

  • Infections and vaccines

    Careful antibiotic use for neonatal sepsis, and the full IAP immunisation schedule for infants and children.
